Safety

Careful introductions

Amanikah does not claim that every introduction is safe. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services now. Amanikah is not an emergency hotline.

What a check can and cannot say

A future ProofPass check, if offered and you request one, looks at one stated claim and the papers supplied for it. It records the date and what was left out. It is not proof that a person is kind, honest in every matter, or a suitable spouse.

Before you share more

  • Send a card or a note only to someone you intend to speak with.
  • Keep financial details, identity documents, and your address out of the first message.
  • Do not send money, gifts, or tickets because of a conversation that began here.
  • Meet in a way your family or a trusted person knows about, when you are ready to meet.

Report a safety concern

Urgent safety reports create a separate ticket for the operator. Success appears only after the server accepts the report. General questions belong on the contact page.
