An idea we're exploring with families

Good introductions begin with people we trust.

We hope to work with masjids and trusted community volunteers to help adults and families begin respectful marriage conversations, close to home and across distances.

Planning stage only. Amanikah has not yet approved or verified any partner masjid, imam or coordinator. There are no active community matchmaking circles, sponsorship payments or charitable funds. Please do not treat any future introduction as a character guarantee.
Masjid Mahfil — proposed

A community circle, by invitation

Our proposed approach starts with a participating community leader who can explain how introductions work. Every adult chooses what to share, with whom and when. Being known locally would not replace identity checks or your own careful questions.

Our intention: basic participation should remain free. No public browsing of private biodata, no automatic family access, and no pressure to accept a match.

Community Access Fund — proposed

Help make introductions affordable for everyone.

Some families can contribute more. Others may need assistance. We would like a transparent way to support the cost of safe introductions, without making anyone's need publicly visible.

No money is being accepted. This is not yet a registered waqf, charitable trust, donation programme or tax-deductible contribution. Any future fund requires independent legal, financial and governance review.

Interested as a family?

Tell us what support would feel useful to you. This is an expression of interest only, not registration for a live matching service.

Interested as a community volunteer?

Masjid committee members, imams and community organisers can speak with Amanikah about a possible pilot. Submitting interest does not establish a partnership.

What matters most

Each person must be an adult, free to say no, and in control of their personal information. Religious leaders may offer guidance, but they cannot replace the consent of either adult. No one should be asked to pay for basic dignity, or to disclose financial hardship publicly.

We are not asking for Aadhaar, photographs, family biodata, donations or payment details. If you would like to discuss a future community-support initiative, use the contact page.
