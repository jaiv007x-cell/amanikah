Help

What you can do today

Some tools work without an account. Waitlist, contact, and safety forms submit to Amanikah’s server. Production accounts are separate from the demo membership.

Membership demo

Use /register and /login for the private demo membership (dashboard, matches, introductions, messages). It is clearly marked DEMO MODE and stores data in this browser only.

Find My Match (waitlist)

The waitlist form validates each field and submits to Amanikah’s server. You receive a unique request reference. Matchmaking is not live yet. Production accounts are at /account/register.html; demo membership stays on /register.

Nikah priorities

The Nikah Priorities quiz covers ten themes — timeline, wedding style, mahr and dowry, family, location, career, household, communication, voluntary faith lifestyle, and first introductions. Your private profile is free. Mahr is explained so it is not confused with dowry.

Al Wali — a gentle guide, wherever you are

Tap Ask Al Wali at the bottom of a page. You can ask about Nikah priorities, family involvement, mahr, living plans and personal safety. The free guided mode works even without signing up. If AI is configured, you can choose to use Groq; it is always optional, and your typed questions are only sent to the provider after you switch on AI. The optional three-step journey checklist stays in your browser. Neither AI nor a checklist decides who you should marry. For personal religious rulings, speak with a qualified scholar.

Invite a wali or trusted family member

With a verified account, open Family Studio. Write a private Wali Brief in your own words, choose exactly what your guest may read, and invite their verified account by email. They must accept before they can see the allowed details. You can withdraw access or delete your brief. This is a family discussion aid, not a religious ruling, a character check, or a decision made for either adult.

#SimpleNikah, voice, and family brief

The #SimpleNikah campaign walks you through language, preferences, privacy, a voluntary pledge, WhatsApp or native share, and downloadable cards. On the homepage you can still take a quick pledge card, record a one-minute introduction, and copy a family brief. The brief picks up quiz answers when they exist on this device.

If a button seems stuck

  • Allow the microphone only if you want to record. Otherwise use “Copy a private note”.
  • If a waitlist or contact submit fails, the page does not show success. Correct the fields or try again.
  • Quiz and pledge tools may still use local storage for drafts on this device.

Anything else can be written on the contact page (support ticket) or the safety report for urgent concerns.
