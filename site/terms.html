Terms

How this site may be used

Last updated 15 November 2026. These terms describe the live site as configured today.

Who it is for

Amanikah is for adults aged 21 and older who are seeking a nikah. You create your own details. A relative may invite you, but may not submit your biodata for you.

What works today

  • Waitlist at /signup.html — submissions are validated on the server, stored for operators, and given a unique reference. Matchmaking is not live.
  • Contact and safety — support and safety tickets are accepted only when the server confirms them.
  • Production accounts at /account/register.html — email verification, private profiles, consent withdrawal, and account deletion.
  • Demo membership at /register — fictional local data, clearly labeled DEMO MODE, never migrated automatically into production.
  • Free tools — Nikah Priorities quiz and #SimpleNikah pledge (drafts may stay in the browser; unlisted pledge links can be revoked on the server).

What is not promised

  • Using Amanikah does not guarantee a marriage, a reply, or a meeting.
  • Operator-mediated introductions are not operational while waitlist mode is on.
  • A document check, when later agreed in writing, covers only the claim and papers named in that check. It is not a judgement of character.
  • Mahr is the bride’s right in the nikah contract. It is not dowry, and the site does not arrange dowry.

Paid services and sandbox billing

The fee schedule on /pricing/ is a sandbox catalog for testing. Live card capture and live fulfillment are not enabled. A sandbox order is not revenue and is not a tax invoice. If a paid service is offered later, fee, scope, and limits are confirmed before work begins.

Your words

You are responsible for what you submit and share. Do not include another person’s private information unless they have agreed.

Voice Rishta and family participation

Only record your own voice and do not impersonate someone else. Harassment, threats, abuse, sexual content, discrimination, and sharing someone else's private information without permission are prohibited. Members may report or block other participants. Repeated or serious violations can lead to review and access restrictions. Recordings are not public, and access to another person's introduction requires permission; no one can guarantee a listener will not record what they hear.

The Family Assistant, when available, offers general discussion help, not a religious ruling, character certification or prediction of marriage success. Users must explicitly opt into external AI processing for each request.

Operator decisions still required

Retention periods for waitlist notes, ticket bodies, and deleted-account audit logs; the choice of transactional email provider; and any live payment or verification launch gates remain operator/legal decisions. Until those are configured, notifications may queue without email delivery.
