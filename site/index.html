Safety guidance

Questions answered clearly

A more thoughtful start.

We believe good expectations begin with honest answers — including what is and is not ready yet.

Is Amanikah a dating app?

No. Amanikah is being developed for Muslim adults intentionally exploring marriage. Free tools are available now, but live introductions are not yet operational.

Can I join without involving my parents?

Yes. Your adult profile belongs to you. You choose if and when relatives participate in future introduction workflows.

What does the #SimpleNikah pledge mean?

It is a voluntary expression of your preferences about a simpler marriage celebration and avoiding dowry demands. It is not evidence of virtue or honesty. Mahr is distinct from dowry.

Does Amanikah verify income or property?

Not automatically. Scoped checks may become available as separately quoted services. No badge should suggest a claim was checked unless a specific review was completed.

Are matches and paid introductions live?

Not yet. The service is in early access. You can create a production account and explore free experiences; live matching and payment fulfillment have not been activated.

Is the readiness quiz free?

Yes. It provides a private reflection on your stated priorities — not a prediction of marriage success or a judgment of faith.
