# Masjid Mahfil & Community Access — honest pilot plan (17 Nov 2026)

## Decision
Build a **voluntary, noncommercial community-interest pilot**, not a public directory, "masjid verified" badge factory, or donation checkout. This is a *proposal*, not an active partner network or registered charitable endowment.

### 1. Community Circle / Masjid Mahfil
- Start with outreach to **willing** mosque committees or community groups in a limited pilot area; never claim endorsement until a signed permission letter identifies the institution, designated volunteer, permissible uses of its name, complaint owner, and revocation process.
- Offer a basic free introduction service **only after** both adults opt in; the coordinator must never claim to certify someone's character, religion, financial resources, legal marital status, or identity on personal acquaintance alone.
- Use plain-language labels: **"Community reference confirmed (scope/date)"** rather than **"Masjid Verified"** unless independent review establishes exactly what is verified; never use a badge to mean safety guaranteed or wealth/status.
- No public biodata listings and no exact mosque-based geolocation of individual users. Neither wali nor coordinator may override an adult's consent, reveal confidential information, or compel the marriage.
- Training: referral scripts, privacy boundaries, consent, discrimination avoidance, impersonation warning, safety escalation, child protection (Amanikah only admits ages 21+), and conflicts of interest.
- Pilot data: store only minimal **expressions of interest** as support tickets, with role, city/town, contact, and request. The public site MUST clearly show that no partnerships or introductions are live.
- Do not publish a mosque name, imam identity, badge or partner metric without explicit written permission and an evidence-based claim.

### 2. Community Access Fund (proposed; NOT a Waqf)
- Keep the free base service free. Financial need cannot be surfaced on member profiles or used to rank, exclude, or shame users.
- Do **not** solicit, accept, pool, hold or route donations, issue charitable receipts, promise tax relief, claim zakat eligibility, charge a charity administrative fee or use the word *waqf* as a representation of legal status until India-qualified counsel and accounting advisers establish the correct entity, trustees, permissions and reporting.
- Indian waqf law was amended in 2025, including documented waqf creation and registration requirements. A consumer-app "Waqf Pool" is not a registered waqf by naming it.
- Foreign contributors require classification under FCRA. An Indian citizen residing abroad may differ from OCI/foreign passport donors; collecting international charity contributions before proper FCRA advice, eligibility and banking permissions is unsafe.
- If later approved, use a ring-fenced ledger with separate balances for donation receipts, approved beneficiary allocations, disbursements, restricted grants, administrative expenses, refunds and reconciliation. No fictitious achievements or spiritually guaranteed claims. Publish audited, aggregated, consent-safe figures only.
- Avoid "Nikah Patron" public member badges by default: they may reveal donation/poverty status, encourage performative giving or rank users by wealth. Optional private acknowledgement can be explored with consent.

### 3. Pilot gates
**Gate A: code and safety** — operator console XSS tested; live API access restrictions tested; early-access contact intake active; prior verified registration still paused while mail disabled; daily human review assigned.
**Gate B: privacy and backup** — documented data lifecycle and approved retention periods; encrypted offsite backup with restore drill; complaints, erasure, family access revocation tested.
**Gate C: real mosque pilot** — legally reviewed cooperation agreement, designated accountable coordinator, training and safeguarding procedure; first verified opt-in invitations with three-account checks; no public matchmaking yet.
**Gate D: charitable program** — separate legal accounting entity, regulatory approvals, finance controls, charity receipts, donor-source verification and independent reporting. Only then consider money movement.
**Gate E: paid services** — separate approved payments, refunds, taxes, fulfilment and complaint operations. Never make paying users outrank safety or remove free essential access.

### 4. Metrics (do not invent)
Review weekly using actual operator records, never made-up case studies:
- Interest requests received by role and city (aggregated, low-count suppression).
- Signed and trained community partners (zero until actually approved).
- Fraction of informed opt-in invitations accepted, declined and revoked.
- Safety response time, unresolved high-risk reports and revocations.
- Free support delivered versus funded grants (zero until actual financial operation).
- Complaints per completed introduction; privacy incidents and restoration drills.
